Whether you want to drive a Mahindra Thar 4x4 with your friends to Gir Forest or hire a chauffeured Mercedes-Benz E-Class to pick up high-profile clients at Ahmedabad Airport, we guarantee spotless execution.

Transparent Kilometer Rates for Taxi & Chauffeur

Unlike app-based aggregators with surging peak multipliers and hidden driver allowances, our Taxi and Chauffeur service operates on transparent, pre-agreed per-kilometer rates. Starting from ₹12/km for sedans to ₹35/km for luxury sedans (Mercedes, BMW, Audi) — you only pay for what you travel.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

1. What documents are required to book a self-drive car?
You only need: (1) A valid Original Driving License (min. 1 year old) and (2) Aadhaar Card or Passport for identity and address verification. Upload via WhatsApp or during delivery.
2. Is there a security deposit?
We offer a Zero Security Deposit Scheme for verified Gujarat residents. For outstation travelers, a nominal refundable deposit of ₹2,000 to ₹5,000 is processed back to your UPI within 2 hours of vehicle return.
3. Can I take the car anywhere in Gujarat?
Yes! All our cars are registered for statewide travel across Gujarat including Somnath, Dwarka, Diu, Kutch, Statue of Unity, and Gir. Out-of-state permits (Rajasthan/Maharashtra) are also available upon request.
4. How does the per-kilometer taxi billing work?
Our chauffeur tracks the opening and closing odometer readings in front of you. You pay the exact agreed per-km rate plus toll & parking. Zero surge pricing, zero night surcharges.
